# Preamble

We believe executives deserve better software.

Most business software generates information.

ORION exists to generate understanding.

Everything built within ORION shall strengthen executive decision-making through intelligence, transparency, and trust.

---

# Article I – Mission

ORION exists to help executives make better decisions.

Every feature, component, service, and interface must support this mission.

---

# Article II – Core Values

ORION values:

- Truth over assumption
- Trust over automation
- Simplicity over complexity
- Insight over information
- Quality over speed
- Transparency over mystery
- Consistency over convenience

---

# Article III – Executive Trust

Executive trust is the most valuable asset of ORION.

Trust is earned through:

- Accurate calculations
- Explainable outcomes
- Reliable operation
- Honest confidence levels
- Consistent behaviour

No feature shall compromise executive trust.

---

# Article IV – Deterministic Core

Core business calculations must be deterministic.

Given the same inputs, ORION shall always produce the same outputs.

Artificial Intelligence may:

- Explain
- Summarize
- Recommend
- Simulate

Artificial Intelligence shall not alter deterministic business calculations.

---

# Article V – Provider Independence

Providers are interchangeable.

Business logic belongs to ORION.

Changing a provider must never require rewriting business rules.

---

# Article VI – Explainability

Every score.

Every alert.

Every recommendation.

Every forecast.

Must be explainable.

The executive shall always understand:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- How it was calculated
- What assumptions were used

---

# Article VII – Architecture

ORION shall remain modular.

Each layer shall have one responsibility.

Dependencies flow downward only.

No circular dependencies.

---

# Article VIII – Engineering

Every implementation shall comply with:

- SOLID
- Clean Architecture
- Dependency Injection
- Strategy Pattern where appropriate
- Comprehensive automated testing

---

# Article IX – Documentation

Architecture is part of the product.

Every significant architectural decision shall be documented.

Engineering Specifications, ADRs, Development Contracts, and Architecture documents must remain current.

---

# Article X – Quality

No feature is complete until:

- TypeScript passes
- Lint passes
- Tests pass
- Coverage targets are met
- Build succeeds
- Documentation is updated

---

# Article XI – Security

Security is a design requirement.

Never expose secrets.

Never trust external inputs.

Validate provider data.

Protect executive information.

---

# Article XII – Scalability

Every architectural decision should support long-term growth across:

- Industries
- Customers
- Providers
- Data volume
- Team size

without requiring architectural redesign.

---

# Article XIII – Human Judgment

ORION augments executive judgment.

It does not replace it.

Final accountability always remains with the executive.

---

# Article XIV – Continuous Improvement

ORION shall evolve continuously.

Improvements should preserve stability while expanding capability.

Backward compatibility should be considered whenever practical.

---

# Oath

Every contributor to ORION shall strive to build software that executives can trust with their most important decisions.

---

# Motto

> "Build software executives can trust."
