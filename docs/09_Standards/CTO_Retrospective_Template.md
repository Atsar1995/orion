# Purpose

The CTO Retrospective captures what was learned during each major phase of ORION.

Unlike Engineering Specifications or Release Records, this document focuses on architectural maturity, engineering quality, product evolution, and strategic lessons.

It exists to ensure ORION continuously improves while preserving its architectural integrity.
