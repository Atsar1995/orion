## Executive Summary

This audit establishes an engineering health baseline before the Enterprise HCM domain (D-014 / P-012.x). The ORION Platform comprises **~1,436 TypeScript source files**, **111 test files**, **184 API routes**, and **709 modules under `lib/`**. The codebase **compiles and builds successfully** but **does not pass full quality gates** — lint reports **1 error**, the test suite reports **18 failures** (703/721 pass, 97.5%).

Architecture patterns are **generally sound** for certified domains (Finance, Hospitality, Commercial, Compliance, Integration, Data Platform Phase I). Significant **technical debt** remains in partial platform missions (Workflow, Search, Document Management), dual intelligence stacks, oversized domain modules, root-level static data files, and documentation drift.

**Engineering Health Score: 68 / 100 (C+)**  
**Certification Decision: CONDITIONAL GO** — HCM blueprint and isolated domain scaffolding may proceed; stabilization remediation required before HCM production implementation.

**Impact:** Duplicated aggregation, types, and data-fetch paths. Documented in [Engineering-Audit-Report.md](./Engineering-Audit-Report.md) (C-01). **Must not be extended by HCM** — consume executive intelligence via public facades only.
