# P-008.6 — Customer Analytics & Relationship Intelligence Platform Architecture

**Mission:** P-008.6 — Customer Analytics & Relationship Intelligence Platform  
**Epic:** P-008 Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform  
**Domain:** D-006 Customer Intelligence  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

The Customer Analytics platform provides unified understanding of customer value, engagement, loyalty, retention, and growth across ORION domains. All profiles anchor on `partyId` from Universal Party — intelligence does not own identity.

**CTO rule:** Operational events (stays, contracts, invoices, activities) feed the intelligence layer. Customer Intelligence owns derived strategic measures: CLV, Loyalty Index, Engagement Score, Retention Risk, and Growth Potential.

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `CustomerProfileService` | Unified profile per partyId with commercial/hospitality/financial summaries |
| `SegmentationEngine` | VIP, Corporate, Enterprise, At Risk, etc. |
| `JourneyAnalyticsService` | Acquisition → Advocacy timeline from operational history |
| `RetentionPredictionService` | Retention risk scoring and recommended actions |
| `GrowthOpportunityEngine` | Account expansion identification |
| `CustomerInsightGenerator` | Strategic insights for Executive Memory |
| `CustomerIntelligenceAnalyticsService` | Executive dashboard and brief signals |

Public facade: `crmCustomerIntelligenceService`

## Journey Model

| Stage | Sources |
|-------|---------|
| Acquisition | Leads |
| Engagement | Opportunities (open stages) |
| Conversion | Won opportunities |
| Service Delivery | Contracts, hospitality stays |
| Renewal | Contract renewals, renewal milestones |
| Advocacy | Relationship milestones |

---

## Derived Metrics

| Metric | Derivation |
|--------|------------|
| Lifetime Value | Won revenue + active contracts + hospitality spend |
| Loyalty Index | Stays, won deals, VIP status |
| Engagement Score | Activities, open pipeline, hospitality frequency |
| Retention Risk | Contract expiry + engagement + stalled deals |
| Growth Potential | Open pipeline, negotiation stage, loyalty |
