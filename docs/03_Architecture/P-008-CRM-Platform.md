**Epic:** P-008 — Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform

**Applicable Canon:** C-001 through C-010  
**Dependencies:** P-001 through P-007.8  

---

## Business Objective

Implement the Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform as the shared customer domain for ORION. The platform manages the complete lifecycle of individuals and organizations across sales, hospitality, commerce, finance, marketing, and future workspaces. Customers exist independently of any single workspace.

---

## CTO Recommendation

The Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform owns **relationships**. Hospitality owns stays. Finance owns accounting. Commerce owns orders. Marketing owns campaigns. HR owns employees. Every workspace references the same customer identity rather than maintaining duplicate records.
