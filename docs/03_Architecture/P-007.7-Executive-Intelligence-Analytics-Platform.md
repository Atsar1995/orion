# P-007.7 — Hospitality Executive Intelligence & Operational Analytics Architecture

**Mission:** P-007.7 — Hospitality Executive Intelligence & Operational Analytics  
**Epic:** P-007 Hospitality Workspace v1.0  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

Transform hospitality operational data into actionable executive intelligence — KPIs, trends, forecasts, insights, and recommendations — continuously enriching the ORION Executive Brief, Decision Intelligence, and Executive Memory.

The objective is not merely to report what happened, but to **explain why**, **predict what is likely next**, and **recommend the most valuable actions**.

---

## Architecture

```
lib/hospitality/analytics/
├── index.ts                 # HospitalityAnalyticsFacade
├── analytics-events.ts      # IIL publisher for Executive Memory
types/hospitality-analytics.ts
lib/hospitality/data/seed-analytics.ts
lib/hospitality/mappers/analytics-brief.ts
```

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `KpiService` | Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, GOP placeholder, cancellation/no-show rates |
| `TrendAnalysisService` | Historical trend narratives |
| `ForecastingEngine` | Occupancy, revenue, staffing, maintenance forecasts |
| `InsightGeneratorService` | Why/predict/recommend narratives |
| `BenchmarkService` | Comp-set benchmark comparison |
| `ExecutiveMetricsService` | Executive dashboard with health scores |
| `OperationalAnalyticsService` | Today's ops, HK progress, maintenance |
| `GuestIntelligenceAnalyticsService` | Repeat/VIP/loyalty/satisfaction |
| `CommercialAnalyticsService` | Sources, segments, conversion, revenue mix |

Public facade: `hospitalityAnalyticsService`
