# P-007.3 — Guest Intelligence & Relationship Platform Architecture

**Mission:** P-007.3 — Guest Intelligence & Relationship Platform  
**Epic:** P-007 Hospitality Workspace v1.0  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

The Guest Intelligence & Relationship Platform is ORION's canonical guest model — the single source of truth for all guest information across Hospitality and future CRM workspaces. Guests exist independently of reservations, enabling lifelong relationship management.

---

## Architecture

```
lib/hospitality/guests/
├── index.ts                 # HospitalityGuestFacade
├── guest-mapper.ts          # GuestRecord ↔ legacy GuestProfile
├── guest-events.ts          # IIL event publisher
types/hospitality-guest.ts   # Canonical domain types
lib/hospitality/data/seed-guests.ts
```

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `GuestService` | CRUD, merge, brief signals |
| `GuestSearchService` | Multi-criteria search |
| `GuestTimelineService` | Communication & history timeline |
| `GuestAnalyticsService` | LTV, repeat rate, satisfaction |
| `PreferenceService` | Guest preference management |
| `RelationshipService` | Family, corporate, agent links |
| `ConsentManagementService` | Marketing & data consent |
| `DuplicateDetectionEngine` | Duplicate detection & merge support |

Public facade: `hospitalityGuestService`

---

## Guest Model

`GuestRecord` is independent of reservations. Reservations reference `guestId` as a foreign key. Guest history aggregates reservation records, timeline entries, communications, and analytics.

---

## Platform Integration

| Platform | Integration |
|----------|-------------|
| Executive Brief | VIP arrivals, repeat guests, satisfaction trends, service recovery |
| Decision Intelligence | VIP recognition, retention, personalized services |
| IIL | `GuestCreated`, `GuestUpdated`, `GuestMerged`, `PreferenceUpdated`, `ConsentUpdated`, `VipFlagged` |
| Reservations (P-007.2) | Validates `guestId` on create; search by reservation number |

---

## CRM Bridge

The `GuestRecord` entity is designed as the foundational business entity for cross-workspace identity. Future CRM modules shall reference the same guest ID rather than maintaining separate customer records.

---

## Reference Implementation Rule

All workspaces must consume `hospitalityGuestService` (or its future platform-level successor) — not maintain parallel guest/customer state.
