# P-008.1 — Universal Party & Organization Model Architecture

**Mission:** P-008.1 — Universal Party & Organization Model  
**Epic:** P-008 Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

The Universal Party & Organization Model is ORION's canonical customer domain — the single source of truth for individuals and organisations across CRM, Hospitality, Commerce, Finance, and future workspaces. Parties exist independently of any single workspace.

---

## Architecture

```
types/crm-party.ts              # Canonical domain types
lib/crm/data/seed-parties.ts    # Seed from CRM customers + extended org types
lib/crm/parties/
├── index.ts                    # CrmPartyFacade
lib/crm/crm-events.ts           # IIL event publisher
lib/crm/repositories/PartyRepository.ts
```

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `OrganisationService` | Organisation CRUD, directory, detail |
| `PersonService` | Individual contact CRUD and detail |
| `PartySearchService` | Cross-party search |
| `PartyRelationshipService` | Employs, partner, subsidiary links |
| `PartyAnalyticsService` | Type/status/role analytics, brief signals |
| `PartyRulesEngine` | Validation rules |

Public facade: `crmPartyService`

---

## Party Model

Supports individuals, organisations, corporate accounts, travel agencies, tour operators, government bodies, suppliers, partners, associations, educational institutions, NGOs, and membership organisations.

`CrmCustomerRecord` links to organisations via `organisationId` — workspaces reference the same party identity rather than duplicating records.

---

## Platform Integration

| Platform | Integration |
|----------|-------------|
| Executive Brief | Party registry counts, active customer relationships |
| Decision Intelligence | Tenant-scoped `ServiceContext` on all operations |
| IIL | `PartyCreated`, `PartyUpdated`, `OrganisationCreated`, `RelationshipLinked`, `PartyArchived` |
| Hospitality (P-007.3) | Guest records remain hospitality-scoped; CRM party model is the cross-workspace successor |
