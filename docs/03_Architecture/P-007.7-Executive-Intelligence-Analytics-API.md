# P-007.7 — Hospitality Executive Intelligence & Analytics API

**Base path:** `/api/hospitality/analytics`  

---

## Full Analytics

### `GET /api/hospitality/analytics`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns complete analytics view: executive, operational, guest, commercial, KPIs, trends, forecasts, insights, benchmarks, recommendations, alerts.

---

## KPIs & Benchmarks

### `GET /api/hospitality/analytics/kpis`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns KPI library and benchmark comparisons.

---

## Forecasts & Trends

### `GET /api/hospitality/analytics/forecasts`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns occupancy/revenue/staffing/maintenance forecasts and historical trends.

---

## Insights & Recommendations

### `GET /api/hospitality/analytics/insights`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns generated insights and prioritized recommendations with rationale.

---

## Executive Intelligence

### `GET /api/hospitality/analytics/executive`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns executive dashboard, hospitality intelligence, and brief contribution.

---

## Operational Analytics

### `GET /api/hospitality/operations`
**Query:** `propertyId`

Returns operational analytics dashboard (arrivals, HK progress, maintenance, rates).

---

## Legacy Intelligence

### `GET /api/hospitality/intelligence`

Returns intelligence result and brief contribution (now powered by analytics engine).

---

|-------|---------|
