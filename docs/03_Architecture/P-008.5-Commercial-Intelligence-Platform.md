# P-008.5 — Commercial Intelligence & Revenue Analytics Platform Architecture

**Mission:** P-008.5 — Commercial Intelligence & Revenue Analytics Platform  
**Epic:** P-008 Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform  
**Domain:** D-005 Commercial Intelligence  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

The Commercial Intelligence Platform transforms commercial activity into executive insight through forecasting, KPIs, relationship scoring, and actionable recommendations. All computations derive from canonical Party, Commercial, and Agreements data — intelligence does not own transactional state.

**CTO rule:** Intelligence explains what is happening, why it is happening, what is likely to happen next, and which executive actions will produce the greatest commercial impact.

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `CommercialKpiService` | Pipeline value, won/lost revenue, win rate, velocity, CLV, relationship health |
| `ForecastEngineService` | Revenue, pipeline, renewal, opportunity forecasts; forecast history |
| `CommercialAnalyticsService` | Pipeline by stage/owner; revenue by industry/territory/account |
| `RelationshipScoringEngine` | Per-account health scores with drivers |
| `InsightGeneratorService` | Explainable insights (what, why, likely next) |
| `CommercialRecommendationEngine` | Priority accounts, pipeline actions, pricing/renewal actions |
| `CommercialIntelligenceAnalyticsService` | Executive dashboard and brief signals |

Public facade: `crmCommercialIntelligenceService`

---

## Forecast Model

| Type | Basis |
|------|-------|
| Revenue | Weighted open pipeline (probability × value) |
| Pipeline | Monthly projection from weighted pipeline |
| Renewal | Active renewal records + contract values |
| Opportunity | Top 5 deals by score |

Forecast history stores projected vs actual values for accuracy tracking (organizational learning).
