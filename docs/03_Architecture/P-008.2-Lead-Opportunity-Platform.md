# P-008.2 — Lead & Opportunity Management Platform Architecture

**Mission:** P-008.2 — Lead & Opportunity Management Platform  
**Epic:** P-008 Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform  
**Domain:** D-002 Commercial Domain  
**Status:** Production-ready (in-memory persistence)  

---

## Purpose

The Lead & Opportunity Management Platform manages the commercial lifecycle from initial interest through successful conversion or closure. Commercial entities reference Universal Party via `partyId` — they do not own customer identity.

**CTO rule:** Leads and opportunities remain independent of reservations. Commercial intent is never confused with operational bookings.

---

## Architecture

```
types/crm-commercial.ts
lib/crm/data/seed-commercial.ts
lib/crm/commercial/
├── index.ts                  CrmCommercialFacade
└── commercial-mapper.ts      Legacy UI bridge
lib/crm/commercial-events.ts
lib/crm/repositories/CommercialRepository.ts
```

### Services

| Service | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| `LeadService` | Lead CRUD, qualification |
| `OpportunityService` | Opportunity CRUD, lead conversion, won/lost |
| `PipelineService` | Stage columns, pipeline metrics |
| `ScoringEngine` | Lead and opportunity scoring |
| `ForecastService` | Revenue forecast, top opportunities |
| `CommercialActivityService` | Sales activities linked to leads/opportunities |

Public facade: `crmCommercialService`

---

## Lifecycle Models

### Lead Sources
Website, Referral, Travel Agent, Corporate, Campaign, Walk-in, Phone, Email, Social Media, Import

### Lead Status
New → Qualified → Contacted → Proposal Requested → Converted | Lost | Archived

### Opportunity Stages
Identified → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won | Lost | Closed

| Platform | Integration |
