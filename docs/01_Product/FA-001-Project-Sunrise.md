# Purpose

The purpose of this assignment is to discover how the Founder naturally thinks while running the business.

This exercise is **not** about software.

It is **not** about screens.

It is **not** about features.

It is about understanding the real questions an executive asks every day.

These questions will become the foundation for ORION's Executive Intelligence Platform.

---

# Vision

ORION is designed to help executives make better decisions.

The best way to achieve this is to understand the actual decision-making process of its first executive user.

Rather than building software around assumptions, ORION will be built around real executive behaviour.

---

# Duration

Seven (7) consecutive business days.

---

# Assignment

For the next seven days, observe yourself while managing your businesses.

Carry a notebook or use your mobile phone.

Every time you ask yourself a business question, record it immediately.

Do not filter the questions.

Do not organize them.

Do not attempt to solve them.

Simply capture them exactly as they occur.

---

# Recording Rules

Write every question exactly as it appears in your mind.

Use natural language.

Avoid technical terminology.

Do not think about ORION while recording.

Do not think about implementation.

Do not think about solutions.

Only record the questions.

---

# Example

Instead of writing

```
Occupancy
```

write

```
How many rooms are vacant this weekend?
```

Instead of writing

```
Payments
```

write

```
Which customers have not paid their invoices?
```

Capture the question—not the topic.

# Guidelines

Record every question.

Small questions matter.

Large questions matter.

Questions that repeat are extremely valuable.

Do not worry about duplication.

Frequency is useful.

---

# Expected Outcome

By the end of the exercise, the Founder is expected to have recorded approximately:

- 100 questions
- 200 questions
- or more

These questions represent genuine executive behaviour and will form the basis of ORION's product design.

---

# CTO Responsibilities

After the exercise, the CTO will:

- Group similar questions.
- Remove duplicates.
- Identify recurring decision patterns.
- Classify questions into Executive Intelligence domains.
- Design Executive Dashboard components.
- Define Business Workspace requirements.
- Create Recommendation Engine rules.
- Create Executive Brief templates.
- Prepare future AI guidance models.

---

# Product Philosophy

Traditional software is built around features.

ORION will be built around executive questions.

Every feature should exist because it answers a real business question.

If no executive question exists, the feature should be challenged before implementation.

---

# Success Criteria

This assignment will be considered successful when:

- Real executive questions have been captured.
- Questions reflect actual daily business behaviour.
- Patterns emerge naturally.
- Repeated questions are identified.
- Product requirements are derived from observations rather than assumptions.
