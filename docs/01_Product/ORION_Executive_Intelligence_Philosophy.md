# Purpose

ORION exists to help executives make better decisions.

ORION does not exist to display data.

ORION does not exist to replace executives.

ORION exists to amplify executive judgment.

---

# The Executive Problem

Executives are overwhelmed.

Every day they receive

- reports
- dashboards
- emails
- meetings
- alerts
- spreadsheets
- notifications

The problem is not lack of information.

The problem is lack of clarity.

# Executive Principles

## Principle 1

Reduce noise.

Increase clarity.

---

## Principle 2

Summarize before expanding.

---

## Principle 3

Highlight exceptions.

Ignore the ordinary.

---

## Principle 4

Focus attention on decisions.

Not metrics.

---

## Principle 5

Explain every recommendation.

Trust comes before automation.

---

## Principle 6

Never overwhelm the executive.

One excellent insight is worth more than one hundred charts.

---

## Principle 7

Confidence matters.

ORION should state when confidence is low.

---

## Principle 8

Context changes everything.

A 5% revenue decline may be catastrophic.

Or completely normal.

Always interpret data within context.

---

## Principle 9

Prioritize.

Executives should immediately know

1. What requires attention?
2. Why?
3. What happens if ignored?
4. Recommended next action.

---

## Principle 10

Respect executive time.

The highest value report is the shortest report that enables the correct decision.

# Human + AI

ORION does not replace executive judgment.

It strengthens it.

AI provides

- explanation
- comparison
- simulation
- recommendation

The executive provides

- experience
- ethics
- intuition
- accountability

Both are required.

---

# Trust Model

Executives should always know

- Where data came from
- How scores were calculated
- Why recommendations were made
- What assumptions were used
- How confident ORION is

Transparency creates trust.
