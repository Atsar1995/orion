## Governance

The ORION Canon v1.0 is the supreme governing document of the platform.

In the event of conflict between any engineering, product, design, AI, architecture, or workspace documentation, the ORION Canon shall prevail.

---

# Purpose

This document defines the immutable principles that govern the design, development, and evolution of ORION.

These principles are intended to outlive technologies, programming languages, frameworks, and trends.

Every product decision shall be evaluated against this constitution.

---

# Vision

ORION is not another business application.

ORION is an Executive Operating System.

Its purpose is to help leaders make better decisions by transforming business data into executive intelligence.

---

# Mission

To provide executives with clarity, confidence, and control through intelligent, explainable, and actionable insights.

---

# Core Belief

Businesses do not succeed because they collect more data.

Businesses succeed because leaders make better decisions.

ORION exists to improve those decisions.

---

# Product Principles

## Principle 1 — Executive First

Every feature must serve executive decision-making.

---

## Principle 2 — Clarity Before Complexity

Complexity belongs inside the platform.

Clarity belongs on the screen.

---

## Principle 3 — Questions Before Features

Every feature must answer a real executive question.

If no question exists, the feature should not exist.

---

## Principle 4 — Recommendations Before Reports

Executives need guidance.

Not endless reports.

---

## Principle 5 — One Truth

Every business metric should have one authoritative source.

Duplicate calculations are prohibited.

---

## Principle 6 — Explainable Intelligence

Every recommendation must explain:

- Why
- How
- What data was used
- Confidence level
- Expected outcome

---

## Principle 7 — Attention is Precious

The dashboard should protect executive attention.

Never overload the user.

---

## Principle 8 — Calm Software

ORION should feel calm.

No unnecessary colours.

No unnecessary notifications.

No unnecessary movement.

Every element should have purpose.

---

## Principle 9 — AI Assists

AI supports executive judgement.

AI never replaces executive responsibility.

---

## Principle 10 — Trust is Everything

Executives must trust every number.

Accuracy is non-negotiable.

---

# Design Philosophy

ORION should feel like a trusted advisor.

Not a reporting tool.

Not an accounting package.

Not a CRM.

Not a hotel management system.

---

# Every Screen Must Answer

- What does the executive need to know?
- Why does it matter?
- What action should be taken?

---

# Every Recommendation Must Answer

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should I do?
- What happens if I do nothing?

---

# Every Metric Must Be

- Accurate
- Current
- Relevant
- Understandable
- Actionable

---

# Every Notification Must

- Be important.
- Be timely.
- Be actionable.

Otherwise, it should not exist.

---

# Executive Experience

An executive should understand the state of the business in less than sixty seconds.

The platform should reduce stress rather than create it.

---

# Long-Term Vision

ORION should become the first application an executive opens every morning and the last application closed at the end of the day.

---

# Product Test

Before implementing any feature, ask:

- Does this improve executive decision-making?
- Does it save time?
- Does it increase clarity?
- Would the Founder use it every day?

If the answer is **No** to all of these, the feature should be reconsidered.
