# Purpose

The purpose of this assignment is to understand how executive decisions are actually made.

FA-001 identified the questions.

FA-002 identifies the decisions.

Questions lead to information.

Information leads to decisions.

ORION exists to improve those decisions.

---

# Vision

Every executive decision follows a pattern.

```
Question

↓

Information

↓

Analysis

↓

Decision

↓

Action

↓

Outcome
```

ORION should eventually support every stage of this process.

---

# Assignment

For the next seven business days, every time you make an important decision, record it.

Do not record only major strategic decisions.

Small operational decisions are equally valuable.

---

# Record the Following

## 1. What happened?

Example

A guest requested an early check-in.

---

## 2. What question did you ask yourself?

Example

Can we accommodate the request without affecting today's arrivals?

---

## 3. What information did you need?

Example

Room availability.

Housekeeping status.

Expected arrivals.

---

## 4. Where did the information come from?

Example

Phone call.

Memory.

WhatsApp.

Excel sheet.

Booking system.

Conversation with staff.

Paper file.

Website.

Email.

---

## 5. What decision did you make?

Example

Approve early check-in.

---

## 6. Why?

Example

Room was already cleaned.

No conflicting reservations.

VIP customer.

---

## 7. What happened afterwards?

Example

Guest appreciated the flexibility.

Excellent review received.

# Example

**Decision**

Approve complimentary airport transfer.

**Reason**

Guest is returning for the fourth time.

**Outcome**

Positive feedback.

Future booking likely.

---

# Another Example

**Decision**

Delay purchasing inventory.

**Reason**

Cash flow is temporarily tight.

**Outcome**

Protected working capital.

---

# Daily Review

At the end of every day ask yourself:

**What were today's three most important decisions?**

Record them.
