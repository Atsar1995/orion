## 1. Vision

Finance is the **Enterprise Financial Operating System** for ORION.

Every business domain generates operational activity — reservations, sales, contracts, payments, and services delivered. Finance transforms those operational events into **financial truth**: validated, balanced, auditable records that executives and regulators can trust.

Finance does not compete with Commercial for customer relationships or with Hospitality for guest experiences. Finance answers a different question: *What is the financial consequence of what the enterprise did, and what does that mean for cash, profit, compliance, and control?*

Through the shared executive platform, Finance provides **trusted financial information for executives** — not as a back-office ledger in isolation, but as a first-class contributor to the Executive Brief, Decision Intelligence, Executive Memory, and enterprise analytics.

Finance is event-aware, organization-scoped, and designed for multi-company, multi-currency, and multi-standard reporting as the enterprise grows.

---

## 2. Mission

Finance records, validates, reconciles, and reports **every financial consequence** generated by enterprise operations.

The Finance Domain exists to ensure that:

- Every material business event that has a financial impact is captured in the general ledger
- Financial records are complete, balanced, and traceable to their originating business context
- Receivables, payables, cash, budgets, and tax obligations are managed with enterprise-grade control
- Financial statements and executive intelligence reflect a single source of financial truth
- Auditors, regulators, and executives can rely on Finance outputs without re-deriving operational data from other domains

Finance succeeds when operational domains can focus on their missions while Finance independently maintains the authoritative financial record of the enterprise.

Finance **owns** the following capabilities and authoritative records:

| **Journal Entries** | Manual and system-generated entries, approval workflow intent |
| **Accounts Receivable** | Customer invoices, credit notes, aging, collections status |
| **Accounts Payable** | Vendor bills, payment obligations, aging, settlement status |
| **Banking** | Bank accounts, statements, reconciliation state |
| **Cash Management** | Cash position, transfers, liquidity snapshots |
| **Budgets** | Budget versions, allocations, variance baselines |
| **Forecasts** | Financial projections derived from ledger and subscribed events |
| **Financial Periods** | Fiscal calendar, period open/close, posting restrictions |
| **Taxes** | Tax codes, tax lines, compliance reporting inputs |
| **Payments** | Outbound payment records and settlement confirmation |
| **Receipts** | Inbound receipt records and allocation to receivables |
| **Financial Statements** | Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, management packs |
| **Cost Centres** | Cost dimension definitions and allocation targets |
| **Profit Centres** | Profit dimension definitions and P&L segmentation |
| **Financial KPIs** | Derived metrics: runway, DSO, DPO, margin, burn, variance |
| **Financial Intelligence** | Executive financial insights, alerts, and brief signals |

Finance owns the **financial interpretation** of business activity. It references operational identifiers from other domains but does not replace their authoritative records.

## 5. Out of Scope

Finance **does not own** the following. These remain authoritative in their respective domains:

| Domain / Area | What Finance Does Not Own |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| **Commercial** | Customers, leads, opportunities, proposals, contracts, commercial agreements, CRM intelligence |
| **Hospitality** | Reservations, guests, properties, inventory, front office, housekeeping operations |
| **HR** | Employees, payroll master data, benefits administration |
| **Procurement** | Purchase requisitions, vendor selection workflow, catalog management |
| **Assets** | Operational asset lifecycle, maintenance, depreciation policy source (operational) |
| **Projects** | Project plans, tasks, deliverables |
| **Marketing** | Campaigns, audiences, marketing spend planning (operational) |
| **Sales** | Pipeline management, quoting workflow, sales activity |

**Integration rule:** Operational domains publish **financial events** (e.g., billing posted, invoice issued, payment received, revenue recognized trigger). Finance subscribes, validates, and converts those events into accounting transactions. Finance never maintains parallel customer, guest, reservation, or employee master records.

## 6. Business Capabilities

### General Ledger

The foundation of financial truth. Finance maintains the chart of accounts, records all journal entries, enforces double-entry balance, and produces the trial balance. All sub-ledgers post through to the general ledger.

### Accounts Receivable

Finance owns customer-facing financial obligations: invoices, credit notes, receipts allocation, and aging. Customer identity references Commercial Party; Finance owns the receivable balance and collection status.

### Accounts Payable

Finance owns vendor-facing financial obligations: bills, payment schedules, aging, and settlement. Vendor master may reference Commercial or Procurement identifiers; Finance owns the payable balance and payment status.

### Bank Reconciliation

Finance matches bank statement lines to internal cash transactions, identifies discrepancies, and maintains reconciliation status. Banking operational connectivity is consumed; reconciliation state is owned by Finance.

### Budgeting

Finance defines budget structures, versions, and allocations by period, cost centre, and profit centre. Budgets provide the baseline for variance analysis and executive control reporting.

### Cash Management

Finance aggregates cash position across accounts, tracks inflows and outflows, and supports liquidity visibility. Cash forecasts combine ledger history with subscribed operational events.

### Revenue Recognition

Finance applies recognition rules to convert operational revenue triggers into compliant revenue journal entries. Commercial and Hospitality publish revenue events; Finance owns recognition timing and ledger posting.

### Expense Management

Finance records and classifies expenses from approved operational events and manual entries. Expense policy enforcement may consume HR or Procurement signals; the financial expense record is owned by Finance.

### Tax Management

Finance maintains tax codes, calculates tax on transactions, and produces compliance reporting inputs. Tax rules and remittance obligations are Finance-owned; operational tax triggers originate in business domains.

### Period Close

Finance controls fiscal period state: open, soft close, hard close. Posting restrictions, accrual entries, and close checklists ensure complete and controlled period-end reporting.

### Financial Reporting

Finance produces statutory and management financial statements, dimensional reports, and executive packs. Reporting derives from ledger truth, not from operational domain databases.

### Executive Financial Intelligence

Finance publishes derived financial KPIs, variance alerts, cash warnings, and brief signals to the executive platform. Operational intelligence remains in Commercial and Hospitality; financial intelligence is Finance-owned.

## 7. Domain Boundaries

### Authoritative Ownership

| Record Type | Authoritative Owner | Finance Role |
|-------------|---------------------|--------------|
| Customer / Party identity | Commercial | Reference only; AR links to party ID |
| Guest / reservation | Hospitality | Subscribe to billing events; no guest master |
| Sales pipeline / contract | Commercial | Subscribe to billing and revenue events |
| General ledger / journal | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| Invoice / receivable balance | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| Bill / payable balance | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| Bank reconciliation | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| Budget / forecast (financial) | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| Financial statements | **Finance** | Authoritative |
| User / organization context | Platform | Consume; org-scoped operations |

### Boundary Rules

1. **Finance owns financial records.** Other domains own operational records.
2. **Finance never duplicates operational ownership.** No parallel CRM, guest, or reservation entities inside Finance.
3. **Cross-domain access is event- and API-based only.** No direct reads of another domain's data store.
4. **Identifiers are shared, not redefined.** Finance references upstream entity IDs; it does not recreate operational master data.
5. **Intelligence separation is preserved.** Operational KPIs stay in source domains; financial KPIs are derived in Finance.

These boundaries align with [ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_v0.3.md](../../11_Governance/Architecture/ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_v0.3.md) Approved Domain Boundaries.

## 8. Domain Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Validation** | Verify subscribed events and manual entries against chart of accounts, period state, and control rules before posting |
| **Posting** | Convert validated business events and approved entries into balanced journal transactions |
| **Balancing** | Ensure sub-ledgers reconcile to the general ledger; debits equal credits at all times |
| **Financial Control** | Enforce approval thresholds, segregation of duties intent, and period restrictions |
| **Compliance** | Support tax, audit, and regulatory reporting requirements from ledger truth |
| **Consolidation** | Aggregate financial results across cost centres, profit centres, and (future) entities |
| **Forecasting** | Project cash, revenue, and expense based on ledger history and forward event signals |
| **Reporting** | Produce financial statements, management reports, and executive packs on defined cadences |

Finance is accountable for the accuracy and completeness of the enterprise financial record. Operational domains are accountable for the accuracy of the events they publish.

## 9. Integration Philosophy

Finance is **event-driven**.

```
┌──────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────┐
│   Hospitality    │     │    Commercial    │
│  (operations)    │     │  (CRM / sales)   │
└────────┬─────────┘     └────────┬─────────┘
         │  business events       │
         └──────────┬─────────────┘
                    ▼
         ┌──────────────────────┐
         │  Integration Layer   │
         │       (IIL)          │
         └──────────┬───────────┘
                    │ subscribed events
                    ▼
         ┌──────────────────────┐
         │   Finance Domain     │
         │  validate · post ·   │
         │  reconcile · report  │
         └──────────┬───────────┘
                    │ brief signals · KPIs
                    ▼
         ┌──────────────────────┐
         │  Executive Platform  │
         └──────────────────────┘
```

**Principles:**

- Business domains **publish** typed financial and operational events through the Intelligence Integration Layer (IIL)
- Finance **subscribes** to approved event contracts and converts them into accounting transactions
- Finance may expose **approved read APIs** for financial balances and statements; other domains do not write to Finance stores directly
- **No direct database integration** between domains — cross-domain interaction occurs only through approved APIs and Enterprise Event Contracts
- Event contracts remain **backward compatible** per Baseline v0.3 freeze; breaking changes require ADR and consumer migration
- Finance registers as a workspace service (`finance-workspace`) with authorized publishers and subscribers per platform standards

**Expected event sources at launch:**

| Source Domain | Example Event Categories |
|---------------|-------------------------|
| Hospitality | Billing posted, folio settled, revenue recognized |
| Commercial | Invoice issued, payment received, contract value recognized |
| Platform | Organization context, audit trail contribution |

## 10. Executive Intelligence

Finance feeds the executive operating layer with **financial truth signals**, distinct from operational intelligence in Commercial and Hospitality.

| Platform Capability | Finance Contribution |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| **Executive Brief** | Cash position, period close status, material variances, receivables/payables alerts, revenue and margin highlights |
| **Decision Intelligence** | Financial recommendations: collection priority, payment timing, budget reallocation options, liquidity actions |
| **Executive Memory** | Fiscal milestones, audit outcomes, major financial decisions, period close history |
| **Analytics** | Trend analysis on financial KPIs; platform composes cross-domain executive views |
| **Executive Dashboard** | Financial KPI widgets: runway, DSO, DPO, gross margin, budget variance, forecast vs actual |

Finance publishes brief signals and intelligence through the IIL using the same executive intelligence profile established by certified domains. Operational metrics from CRM or Hospitality are not re-published by Finance; Finance publishes **financial derivatives** only.

## 11. Architectural Principles
### Financial Event Sourcing
Every posted accounting transaction shall be traceable to one or more immutable business events published through the Intelligence Integration Layer (IIL).

Financial records shall always preserve complete event lineage from the originating business event to the final ledger posting.

No financial transaction shall exist without an auditable source event.

| Principle | Statement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Double Entry Accounting** | Every transaction balances; the ledger is always in equilibrium |
| **Single Source of Financial Truth** | One general ledger; no shadow accounting in operational domains |
| **Immutable Ledger** | Posted entries are not deleted; corrections occur through reversing entries |
| **Event Driven** | Business events drive posting; Finance reacts to enterprise activity |
| **Audit First** | Traceability from journal line to source event is mandatory |
| **API First** | Cross-domain interaction through approved contracts, not shared databases |
| **Organization Scoped** | All financial records scoped by `organizationId` via Platform Organization |
| **Multi Company Ready** | Design supports multiple legal entities within an organization |
| **Multi Currency Ready** | Design supports transaction, functional, and reporting currencies |
| **Tax Ready** | Tax dimensions and compliance reporting are first-class concerns |
| **AI Ready** | Structured financial data and explainable KPIs support future AI-assisted forecasting and anomaly detection |
| **Executive Intelligence Ready** | Financial intelligence facades integrate with Brief, Memory, and Decision services |

Finance consumes shared platform services (Identity, Organization, IIL, Brief, Memory, Decision, Audit, Search, Notifications, Analytics) and does not reimplement them.

## 12. Success Criteria

Finance Domain Blueprint approval and subsequent engineering shall be measured against:

| Criterion | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **Boundary compliance** | Zero authoritative operational entities owned by Finance; all cross-domain flows via IIL or approved APIs |
| **Ledger integrity** | 100% of posted journals balance; trial balance always nets to zero |
| **Event coverage** | All material Hospitality and Commercial financial events produce corresponding Finance transactions |
| **Traceability** | Every journal entry links to source event or approved manual entry reference |
| **Executive integration** | Finance brief signals appear in Executive Brief when material thresholds are met |
| **Period control** | Posting blocked in closed periods; period close checklist completable |
| **Reporting accuracy** | Financial statements reconcile to general ledger without manual adjustment |
| **Canon compliance** | All applicable Canon chapters addressed in engineering specifications |
| **Certification readiness** | Domain passes typecheck, lint, test, and build gates before CONDITIONAL GO |
| **Reference designation** | Finance established as enterprise reference for accounting and financial intelligence patterns |
