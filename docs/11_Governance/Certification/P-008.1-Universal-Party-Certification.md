# P-008.1 — Universal Party & Organization Model Certification

**Mission:** P-008.1 — Universal Party & Organization Model  
**Epic:** P-008 Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Decision:** **GO**  

---

## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.1 establishes the Universal Party & Organization Model as ORION's canonical business identity layer. Individuals and organisations exist independently of any workspace; roles determine participation across Hospitality, Finance, Commerce, HR, and Marketing. The platform delivers party management, organisation registry, multi-role assignment, relationship exploration, duplicate detection, identity merge, and external identifier linking — all integrated with Executive Brief, Decision Intelligence, and IIL.

No workspace owns Party. Every workspace references Party.

## Domain Model

| Entity | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `PartyRecord` | Base identity (person or organisation) |
| `PersonRecord` | Individual party |
| `OrganisationRecord` | Organisation party with type classification |
| `PartyRoleType` | 12 workspace-agnostic roles (guest, customer, corporate_contact, travel_agent, tour_operator, supplier, vendor, employee, investor, partner, prospect, member) |
| `PartyRelationship` | Directed graph edges between parties |
| `PartyAddress` | Structured addresses |
| `PartyContactMethod` | Email, phone, website, etc. |
| `PartyCommunicationPreference` | Channel preferences |
| `PartyExternalIdentifier` | Cross-workspace identity linking |
| `PartyRoleAssignment` | Role history with workspace scope |
| `PartyTimelineEntry` | Executive memory timeline |
