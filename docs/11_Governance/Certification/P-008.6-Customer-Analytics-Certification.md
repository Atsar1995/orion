## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.6 delivers the enterprise source of truth for long-term customer value and strategic relationship management. Unified profiles on `partyId` combine commercial, agreement, and hospitality enrichment into derived strategic metrics — separate from raw operational events per CTO guidance.

---

## Business Summary

Executives gain VIP visibility, retention risk alerts, growth opportunities, and segment analysis. Customer journey timelines span acquisition through advocacy, feeding Executive Brief, Decision Intelligence, and Executive Memory.

---

## Customer Intelligence Architecture

Canonical types in `types/crm-customer-intelligence.ts`. The `CrmCustomerIntelligenceFacade` exposes seven services. Profiles computed from Party + Commercial + Agreements + hospitality enrichment bridge.

---

## Journey Model

Six journey stages populated from leads, opportunities, contracts, hospitality enrichment, and relationship milestones. Operational events feed the timeline; intelligence layer owns derived scores.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Hospitality guest ↔ party not fully linked | Enrichment bridge in seed; full link deferred |
| Legacy CrmCustomerRecord coexists | Party profiles are authoritative for P-008.6 |
| P-008.4 engagement not certified | Activity count from commercial activities stub |
| Event subscribers for InvoicePaid etc. | Deferred to platform integration phase |

---

## Recommendations

1. Link Hospitality `GuestRecord` to `partyId` for live operational feed.
2. Wire IIL subscribers for ContractSigned, ReservationCompleted to auto-refresh profiles.
3. Migrate `/crm/customers` directory to consume unified profiles.
4. Add persistent storage for relationship milestones and journey events.
