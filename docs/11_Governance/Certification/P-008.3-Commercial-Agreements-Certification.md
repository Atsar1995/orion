---

## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.3 delivers the authoritative Commercial Agreements Platform for ORION. The platform manages proposals, quotations, contracts, rate agreements, approval workflows, and renewals with full lifecycle traceability. Executed agreements are immutable; amendments create new versions preserving negotiation and approval history per CTO guidance.

---

## Business Summary

Hospitality, Commerce, Finance, and future workspaces can consume approved commercial agreements from this platform rather than maintaining independent pricing and contract logic. The platform surfaces contracts awaiting approval, upcoming renewals, expiring agreements, and pipeline value under contract for Executive Brief and Decision Intelligence.

---

## Commercial Agreement Architecture

Canonical entities live in `types/crm-agreements.ts` with tenant-scoped persistence via `AgreementsRepository`. The `CrmAgreementsFacade` exposes seven domain services. Seed data includes 3 proposals, 2 quotations, 4 contracts, 3 rate agreements, 3 approvals, and 2 renewals for `org-orania`.

## Recommendations

1. Wire Hospitality rate lookup to `crmAgreementsService.rates` for approved corporate/seasonal rates.
2. Add persistent storage adapter implementing `AgreementsRepository`.
3. Integrate Document Service for contract attachment storage.
4. Extend Decision Intelligence with renewal priority scoring from agreement analytics.
