**Mission:** P-008.5 — Commercial Intelligence & Revenue Analytics Platform

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Decision:** **GO**  

---

## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.5 delivers the authoritative Commercial Intelligence layer for ORION. The platform transforms canonical commercial data into executive KPIs, multi-type forecasts, relationship scoring, explainable insights, and actionable recommendations — unified on a single data path from Party, Commercial, and Agreements domains.

---

## Business Summary

Executives gain a consolidated view of pipeline health, revenue forecast, commercial risks, and growth opportunities. Intelligence explains not only what is happening but why, what is likely next, and which actions produce the greatest impact per CTO guidance.

---

## Commercial Intelligence Architecture

Canonical types in `types/crm-commercial-intelligence.ts`. The `CrmCommercialIntelligenceFacade` exposes seven services computing from `CommercialIntelligenceRepository`. Seed includes forecast history, benchmarks, and commercial alerts for `org-orania`.

---

## Forecast Model

Four forecast types (revenue, pipeline, renewal, opportunity) computed from weighted pipeline, renewal records, and top-scored deals. Forecast history enables accuracy tracking (Q1/Q2 2026 seed with 94–95% accuracy). `runForecast()` publishes `ForecastUpdated` to IIL and stores snapshots.

## Recommendations

1. Complete P-008.4 to enrich activity effectiveness and follow-up compliance KPIs.
2. Wire event subscribers for OpportunityWon/Lost and ContractSigned to auto-recalculate forecasts.
3. Retire legacy `CrmIntelligenceEngine` dual data path once P-008.5 is default for `/crm/insights`.
4. Add persistent storage for forecast snapshots and commercial alert history.
