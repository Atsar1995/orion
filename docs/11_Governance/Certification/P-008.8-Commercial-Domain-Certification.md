## Recommendations

1. **Database persistence** — highest priority before production deployment at scale (R-001)
2. **Complete P-008.4** — Activities, Tasks & Communications platform before external domain consumption of activity APIs (R-002)
3. **Unify customer data path** — deprecate legacy `CrmCustomerRecord` in favour of party-anchored profiles (R-003)
4. **Wire executive brief composition** — integrate P-008.7 signals into platform Morning Brief (R-004)
5. **RBAC enforcement** — activate `CRM_ROUTE_PERMISSIONS` when ES-009 ships (R-005)
6. **Cross-workspace event consumers** — Finance and Hospitality subscribe to commercial IIL events
7. **E2E Playwright suite** — browser-level journey tests for certification regression
