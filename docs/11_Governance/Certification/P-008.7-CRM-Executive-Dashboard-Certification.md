## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.7 delivers the primary executive workspace for commercial performance, customer relationships, and revenue growth. The dashboard composes commercial and customer intelligence into an exception-first interface with drill-down from enterprise KPIs to individual contracts, opportunities, and customer profiles.

---

## Dashboard Architecture

Composition facade over P-008.5 + P-008.6 + P-008.3. No duplicate transactional state. Alert management prioritizes critical and high-severity exceptions per CTO guidance.

---

## Business Value

Executives gain unified pipeline, forecast, CLV, and relationship health visibility with proactive alerts for at-risk accounts, stalled opportunities, renewal deadlines, and revenue variance.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Legacy `/crm` overview overlaps | Executive dashboard is canonical P-008.7 surface |
| Activity data split (legacy + commercial) | Both sources merged in activity summary |
| Real-time refresh not event-driven | IIL events published; subscribers deferred |

---

## Recommendations

1. Redirect `/crm` overview to executive dashboard or deprecate legacy KPI cards.
2. Wire IIL subscribers for live alert refresh on contract and retention events.
3. Persist dashboard snapshots to Executive Memory on daily schedule.
4. Add platform Brief integration for unified Daily Commercial Brief.
