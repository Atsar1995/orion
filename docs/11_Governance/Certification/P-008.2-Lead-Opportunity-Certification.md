# P-008.2 — Lead & Opportunity Management Certification

**Mission:** P-008.2 — Lead & Opportunity Management Platform  
**Epic:** P-008 · Domain D-002 Commercial  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Decision:** **GO**  

---

## Executive Summary

Mission P-008.2 delivers the Commercial Domain's lead and opportunity management capability. The platform manages the full commercial lifecycle — from inbound lead through qualified opportunity to won/lost closure — while referencing Universal Party for identity. Commercial intent is strictly separated from operational reservations per CTO guidance.

---

## Architecture

Canonical commercial entities live in `types/crm-commercial.ts` with tenant-scoped persistence via `CommercialRepository`. The `CrmCommercialFacade` exposes lead, opportunity, pipeline, scoring, forecast, and activity services. Legacy CRM opportunity UI continues to function through `commercial-mapper.ts`.
