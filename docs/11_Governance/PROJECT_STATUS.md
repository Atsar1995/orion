# ORION Enterprise Platform — Project Status

**Title:** ORION Enterprise Platform  
**Current Version:** Baseline v0.3  
**Last Updated:** 30 July 2026  
**Authority:** Chief Enterprise Architect  

---

## Status Overview

The ORION platform has reached **Architecture Baseline v0.3**. Core executive capabilities and two certified business domains are operational. The platform is progressing according to architecture.

---

## Completed

### Canon (C-001 through C-010)

The ORION Canon v1.0 is ratified and frozen. All certified domains comply with applicable canon chapters. Canon compliance is a mandatory gate for domain certification and release.

### Core Executive Platform

The shared executive operating layer is implemented and integrated:

- Executive Brief
- Decision Intelligence
- Executive Memory
- Command Center and Advisor surfaces
- Identity and Organization foundations
- Intelligence Integration Layer (IIL)

### Hospitality Domain (Certified)

The Hospitality Workspace (P-007.1 through P-007.8) is **CONDITIONAL GO** certified as the ORION Reference Workspace. End-to-end hospitality operations — property, reservations, guests, front office, housekeeping, billing, and analytics — are validated and integrated with the executive platform.

### Commercial Domain (Certified)

The Customer Relationship & Revenue Platform (P-008.1 through P-008.8) is **CONDITIONAL GO** certified as the enterprise reference implementation for customer, sales, and commercial capabilities. Universal Party, lead and opportunity management, commercial agreements, intelligence, customer analytics, and the executive dashboard are validated and integrated.

---

## Next

### Finance Domain

Finance is the next domain to enter active engineering under Phase IV. Domain blueprints, engineering specifications, API contracts, and data contracts will be established in `docs/Finance/` before implementation begins.

## Overall Status

**Platform progressing according to architecture.**

Baseline v0.3 freezes the current platform state before Finance Domain work begins. No changes to certified domain boundaries or shared platform contracts are permitted without governance review and an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
